# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description

The Simple Interest Calculator is a basic application that calculates the simple interest based on the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula

Simple Interest (SI) is calculated using:

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:

* P = Principal Amount
* R = Rate of Interest per year (%)
* T = Time Period in years

## Example

If:

* Principal = ₹10,000
* Rate of Interest = 5%
* Time = 2 years

Then:

SI = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100

SI = ₹1,000

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

Total Amount = ₹10,000 + ₹1,000 = ₹11,000

## Features

* Calculates simple interest quickly.
* Accepts principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.
* Displays the calculated simple interest.
* Simple and easy-to-use calculator.

## Input

The calculator requires three values:

1. Principal Amount
2. Rate of Interest
3. Time Period

## Output

The calculator displays the calculated Simple Interest.

## How to Use

1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the rate of interest.
3. Enter the time period in years.
4. Click the Calculate button.
5. The Simple Interest will be displayed.
